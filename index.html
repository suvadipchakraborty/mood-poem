Mood Poem

An emotional compass pointing to classic literature. Find the perfect verse for exactly how you feel right now.

Features

  • Keyword-based poetry curation Every mood and weather maps to words that poets have used for centuries, so the match is made line by line.
  • Beautifully formatted stanzas Verses keep their original line breaks and stanza spacing on a card made for slow reading.
  • Native sharing Send a poem to anyone with your phone's own share sheet.

Keep it close

Install Mood Poem to open it from your home screen, even when your connection is shaky.
